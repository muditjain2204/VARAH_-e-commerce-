# 🛍️ MAA LAXMI Store Owner’s Manual
*A simple, non-technical guide to managing sarees, tracking orders, and receiving payments.*
---
## 1. How to Log in to Your Store Manager
Your website has a private, secure Admin Portal built for you. You can open it on your **phone, tablet, or laptop**:

1. Open your browser and go to:
   👉 **`https://maalaxmistore.vercel.app/admin`** *(or click "Store Admin" in the website footer)*.
2. Enter your secret Store Passcode:
   👉 **`MAALAXMI@2026`**
3. Click **"Unlock Admin Portal"**.
4. You are now inside your store management dashboard!
*Your session stays active securely for 7 days so you do not have to keep retyping it unless you click "Exit Admin".*

---

## 2. How to Add a New Saree (Takes 2 Minutes)

Whenever new saree stock arrives at your shop:

1. Click on **"Products"** in the sidebar, then click the blue **"+ Add New Saree"** button (`/admin/products/new`).
2. Fill in the simple details:
   * **Saree Title:** e.g., *Pure Kanjeevaram Silk Saree with Gold Zari*
   * **Category:** Choose from the dropdown (*Banarasi, Silk, Cotton, Chiffon, Kanjivaram, Bridal, etc.*)
   * **Fabric & Color:** e.g., *Silk / Crimson Red*
   * **Occasion:** e.g., *Wedding & Festive*
   * **Description:** A brief sentence describing the border, weave, or pallu work.
3. **Set the Pricing:**
   * **Original MRP (Base Price):** e.g., `₹12,999`
   * **Discounted Sale Price:** e.g., `₹9,999` *(The website will automatically calculate and display the discount badge like "23% OFF")*.
4. **Upload Saree Photos:**
   * Click **"Upload from Computer/Phone"** and select clear photos from your camera or gallery.
5. **Stock Quantity:**
   * Enter how many pieces you have in stock (e.g., `5`).
6. Click **"Publish Saree"**!

> 🎉 **Done!** The new saree appears on your website immediately, ready for customers to buy.

---

## 3. How to Update Prices, Stock, or Mark "Out of Stock"

* **Sold Out?** Open **Products**, find the saree, and change its tag from **"In Stock"** to **"Out of Stock"**. The website will prevent buyers from ordering it until you restock.
* **Price Changes or Festive Sales:** You can update the price anytime with one click.
* **Want it on the Homepage?** Toggle the **"Best Seller"** or **"Trending"** switch on any saree to highlight it on the store’s front page.

---

## 4. How Orders & Customer Details Work

When a customer buys a saree:

1. Go to **"Orders"** (`/admin/orders`) in your Admin Portal.
2. You will see:
   * Customer’s **Full Name, Mobile Number, and Email**
   * Complete **Delivery Address & Pincode**
   * Sarees ordered and total amount paid
   * **Payment Status:** Marked as **"PAID (UPI / Card)"** or **"COD"**
3. Pack the saree and ship it to the customer's address!

---

## 5. How Payments Reach Your Bank Account

**You do NOT need to do anything manually.** 

* When a customer pays via **Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, RuPay, or Credit/Debit Cards**:
  1. The money is securely processed by **Razorpay**.
  2. Razorpay automatically transfers the money **directly into your verified bank account** (typically the next business day).
  3. You can log in to **[dashboard.razorpay.com](https://dashboard.razorpay.com)** anytime to view your daily settlement amounts and bank statements.
